*Bootstrapping of interest rates is a method used to price fixed rate and floating rate instruments consistently. In this methodology, the discounted sum of bond coupon payments equals the discounted sum of the expected short-term interest rates. In fixed income analytics, a discount factor can be derived from the observed yield curve, and expected short-term rates can then be inferred from ratios of adjacent discount factors.
